    1. Arrow Keys / WASD Keys / Swipe to move letters.
    2. Press Enter to submit words when highlighted.
    3. When stuck, click bomb power-up button and select a letter to detonate a 2x2 grid.
    4. Toggle on and off "Show Ready Word" to enable/disable highlight.
    5. Wildcard(?) spawns randomly on the grid. When part of a valid word, click the tile
      directly to choose any letter to complete your word.
    6. Rare double-letter tiles (like EE, OO, TT) help build longer, higher-scoring
      words quickly.
    7. Rare letters (Q, Z, X, J, K, V) grant a 2x Score Multiplier to the word.
    8. Forming a horizontal and vertical word that intersect triggers a Cross Multiplier
      that doubles the combined score of both words.
    9. Back-to-back Cross matches increases your Combo Multiplier (2x, 4x, etc.).
    10. A designated row or column becomes a "Hot Zone" every 5 moves. Clearing a word
      containing a tile inside the Hot Zone awards 2x Bonus Points.
